Meet the Owners

Robert Delp & Brandy Ferguson

We are Cody and Brandy — two young people who grew up in simple, hardworking households where you learned early on that nothing worth having comes without effort. We didn't come from money or connections. We came from a love of honest work, the satisfaction of doing something right, and the belief that if you put your whole self into what you do, good things follow.

B.C. Builders is the product of that drive. We started this company because we wanted to build something of our own — something we could be proud of — and because we saw a real need on the Eastern Shore for quality concrete and engraving work done by people who actually care about the outcome.

Our faith in Jesus keeps us grounded and our values guide the way we do business. We treat every customer the way we'd want to be treated — with honesty, respect, and genuine care. When you hire B.C. Builders, you're not just hiring a contractor. You're working with family.

We'd love the opportunity to earn your trust and your business, and we hope that whatever project brings you to us is just the start of a long and positive relationship.
