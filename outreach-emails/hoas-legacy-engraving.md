**Custom concrete engraving** lets you permanently add your community name, founding year, decorative borders, neighborhood logos, or resident recognition directly into existing concrete — entrance aprons, community walkways, clubhouse areas, and common spaces.

**A few ideas that work well for HOAs:**

- **Community entrance engraving** — neighborhood name and founding year engraved into the entrance apron or pillars for a sharp, polished look that no sign post can match
- **Resident legacy program** — homeowners can make a donation to the HOA in exchange for a name or family dedication engraved along a community walkway, turning common areas into a community keepsake and a fundraising opportunity
- **Clubhouse and common area details** — decorative patterns, community mottos, or milestone dedications engraved into pool decks, gathering areas, or walkways
- **Street and address markers** — custom engraving for community signage in concrete

For HOAs looking to raise funds for common area improvements, a resident legacy program is a straightforward option — residents purchase engraving spaces, proceeds go to the HOA reserve or improvement fund, and we handle all the work. You keep the donations and pay only for engraving.
