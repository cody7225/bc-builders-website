**Subject:** A Lasting Way to Honor Your Members & Raise Funds — Legacy Engraving for Fire Companies

---

Dear [Chief / Fire Company Leadership],

My name is Robert Delp with **B.C. Builders L.L.C.**, a concrete construction and engraving company serving the Eastern Shore. I'm reaching out because I think there's a unique fundraising opportunity that's a natural fit for volunteer fire companies — and it requires no upfront cost to your organization.

**The idea: turn your existing concrete into a lasting tribute and a fundraising tool.**

Your station's apron, walkways, and gathering areas are surfaces your members and community walk past every day. Through a **Legacy Engraving Program**, community members, families, and local businesses can make a donation to your fire company in exchange for having their name, a loved one's name, a badge number, or a tribute message permanently engraved right there on your property.

It's a way to honor the people who support your mission — and a way to fund the equipment, training, and facility improvements that keep your crew ready.

**Some examples of what works well:**

- Fallen member memorials and line-of-duty tributes engraved near the entrance
- Donor recognition with names and years of service along the station walkway
- Community supporter panels for local businesses and families who give
- Apparatus bay apron engravings honoring founding members or milestones

**How it works for your fire company:**

1. You identify the concrete spaces you want to make available — walkways, apron, courtyard.
2. You set the donation tier per space — we can help you structure pricing.
3. We do all the engraving work once donations are collected.
4. Your fire company keeps 100% of the donations. You pay only for the engraving itself.

Volunteer fire companies do so much for this community, and this is a way for the community to give something back that lasts. A name in concrete outside your station carries a lot more weight than a name on a paper certificate.

We serve the Eastern Shore and are willing to travel within about a two-hour radius. You can see examples of our work at **bcbuildersmd.com**.
