We engrave directly into existing concrete — walkways, entrance slabs, gathering areas, columbarium walls, and memorial pads — as well as custom stone work for individual memorials and markers.

**Ways we can help your cemetery:**

- **Donor recognition walkways** — families and community members who contribute to cemetery upkeep or expansion can have their name permanently engraved along an entrance path or garden walkway, giving your fundraising a tangible, lasting result
- **Memorial path engravings** — names, dates, and tributes engraved into concrete paths through the grounds as a dignified alternative or complement to traditional markers
- **Entrance and chapel area detailing** — scripture, dedications, or founding dates engraved into concrete near the entrance or chapel
- **Custom stone memorial work** — headstones, flat markers, and memorial stones engraved and finished with stain and epoxy

**For cemetery boards looking to raise funds for maintenance or future development**, a Legacy Engraving Program is a low-cost, high-impact option. Families purchase engraving spaces on walkways or gathering areas, and the proceeds go directly to the cemetery. We handle all the engraving — you keep the donations and pay only for the work.

We serve the Eastern Shore and travel within about a two-hour radius. You can view examples of our engraving and stone work at **bcbuildersmd.com**.
