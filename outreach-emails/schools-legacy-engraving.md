**The idea: sell engraving space in the concrete your school already owns.**

Sidewalks, courtyards, entrance paths, and athletic areas are surfaces students, staff, and families walk past every year. Through a **Legacy Engraving Program**, families, alumni, and local supporters can donate to the school in exchange for having a name, graduation year, a motivational message, or a class dedication permanently engraved right on school property.

It's a fundraiser with a permanent result — and a tradition that can grow year after year.

**Some ideas that work well for schools:**

- Senior class dedications engraved in the main entrance walkway each graduating year
- Family and alumni donor recognition along courtyard or athletic area paths
- Booster club supporter panels near the gym, stadium, or field entrance
- Memorial tributes for beloved teachers, coaches, or students

**How it works:**

1. Your school or booster club identifies available concrete spaces.
2. You set the donation amount per engraving — we can help you price it.
3. We do all the engraving once donations are collected.
4. Your organization keeps 100% of the donations. You pay only for the engraving work.

This works especially well as an annual senior class tradition — each class leaves their mark on the school permanently, and families are proud to contribute to something that will be there for decades.
