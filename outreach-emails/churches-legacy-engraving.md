Dear [Pastor / Church Office],

My name is Robert Delp with **B.C. Builders L.L.C.**, a concrete construction and engraving company serving the Eastern Shore. I wanted to reach out with an idea that a number of churches have found to be a powerful and lasting way to raise funds for future development and ministry growth.

**The concept is simple: sell the concrete you already own.**

Your sidewalks, entrance paths, courtyard, and gathering areas are more than just surfaces — they're spaces your congregation walks through every single day. Through a **Legacy Engraving Program**, members and families can donate to the church in exchange for having their name, a loved one's name, a Bible verse, or a meaningful message permanently engraved in that concrete.

This gives donors something they can see, point to, and be proud of every time they visit. It turns a donation into a lasting legacy — and it turns your existing property into a fundraising opportunity that costs the church nothing to set up.

**Some ideas we've helped bring to life:**

- Family names or memorial dedications engraved along a walkway leading to the entrance
- Bible verses engraved into courtyard or gathering area concrete
- Donor recognition panels near the main entrance or fellowship hall
- Memorial stones for loved ones with names, dates, and scripture

**How it works for the church:**

1. You decide the size and location of the engraving spaces available.
2. You set the donation amount per space — we can help you price it appropriately.
3. We handle all the engraving after donations are collected.
4. The church keeps 100% of the donations. We charge only for the engraving work itself.

It's a dignified, beautiful alternative to bake sales and car washes — and the results are permanent. Families who donate become visibly woven into the fabric of the church for generations.

We work throughout the Eastern Shore and are willing to travel within about a two-hour radius. You can see examples of our engraving work at **bcbuildersmd.com**.
