**The idea: engrave sponsor and donor names permanently into the concrete at your facility.**

Walkways, dugout aprons, concession stand pads, pavilion floors, and parking lot entrances are concrete surfaces you already own. Through a **Sponsor and Legacy Engraving Program**, local businesses and families can purchase a permanent engraving in exchange for a sponsorship donation — giving your league or park a meaningful, lasting way to recognize the people who make it all possible.

**What works well for leagues and parks:**

- **Sponsor recognition walkways** — local business logos or names engraved along the path to the field, pavilion, or bleachers
- **Player and family legacy panels** — families donate in honor of a player or season and get a name engraved near the dugout or entrance
- **Championship and milestone dedications** — season titles, founding years, or record-setting moments engraved into the facility permanently
- **Concession stand or pavilion branding** — league name, mascot, or logo engraved into the concrete pad
- **Memorial tributes** — honoring coaches, players, or community members who meant something to the program

**How it works:**

1. You identify available concrete spaces at your facility.
2. You set sponsorship tiers — bronze, silver, gold if you want, or a flat rate per space.
3. We do all the engraving once sponsorships are collected.
4. Your league or park keeps 100% of the sponsorship money. You pay only for the engraving work.

It's a sponsorship model with a permanent deliverable — and something sponsors are proud to show off to their customers and community.

**And if a full sponsorship program isn't the right fit** — we can just as easily engrave your own league or park's logo, name, or mission statement directly into your concrete as a straightforward paid job, no donor program needed. A lot of organizations like having their own mark permanently on display at the entrance, pavilion, or walkway even without the fundraising angle.
