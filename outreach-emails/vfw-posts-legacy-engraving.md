**Subject:** Honor Your Veterans Permanently — Legacy Engraving for VFW Posts

---

Dear [Post Commander / Leadership],

My name is Robert Delp with **B.C. Builders L.L.C.**, a concrete construction and engraving company serving the Eastern Shore. I'm reaching out because I believe there's no more fitting place for a permanent tribute than right outside a veterans post — and I'd like to help make that happen.

**The idea: turn your post's concrete into a lasting memorial and fundraising opportunity.**

Your walkways, entrance areas, and gathering spaces see members and community visitors every day. Through a **Legacy Engraving Program**, veterans, families, and community supporters can make a donation to your post in exchange for having a name, rank, branch of service, years served, or a tribute message permanently engraved in the concrete on your property.

It's a way to honor the men and women who served — and raise funds to keep your post running and supporting veterans in the community.

**Examples of what works well:**

- Veteran name panels with branch, rank, and years of service along entrance walkways
- Memorial tributes for fallen members engraved near the post entrance
- Family and community donor recognition for those who support veterans' causes
- Unit or campaign dedications honoring specific service histories

**How it works for your post:**

1. You identify concrete spaces to make available — walkways, courtyard, parking apron.
2. You set donation tiers per engraving space — we can help structure this.
3. We complete all engraving once funds are collected.
4. Your post keeps 100% of the donations. You pay only for the engraving itself.

A name in concrete outside a veterans post is a tribute that doesn't fade, doesn't get filed away, and doesn't get thrown out. It's there for every member who walks past, every year, for generations.

We serve the Eastern Shore and travel within about a two-hour radius. See our work at **bcbuildersmd.com**.
