Concrete Engraving

Custom logos, text, and designs cut directly into concrete — permanent, professional, and built to stand out.

What We Do

Update, Decorate, or Brand Any Concrete Surface

You don't need new concrete to get engraving done. We can work on any existing cured surface — a driveway that's already down, a patio from 10 years ago, a warehouse floor, a sidewalk slab. Whatever concrete is already there, we can engrave it.

No demolition. No replacement. Just professional results cut permanently into what's already there — then filled with colored epoxy to make it pop, or left with a clean natural finish.

Most Popular Opportunity

Fundraising — Sell Space in Concrete

Engraved concrete is one of the most underused fundraising tools out there. Schools, churches, nonprofits, sports organizations, and community groups can sell engraved spaces in a walkway, courtyard, or plaza — and donors get something permanent in return.

Donor Recognition

Engrave donor names, family names, or memorial tributes into walkway bricks or a dedicated slab. Give people something that lasts forever.

Advertising Tiles

Sell engraved squares or sections to local businesses — logo, name, and URL cut right into the concrete. A permanent ad that doesn't need to be renewed.

Legacy Campaigns

Capital campaigns, memorial walks, anniversary projects — sell engraved spots as part of a larger fundraising push. People pay more for something permanent.

Safety & Compliance

Safety Markings That Never Fade

Paint fades. Floor tape peels. Stickers crack. Engraved safety markings stay sharp and readable year after year with zero maintenance — because they're cut directly into the concrete, not applied on top of it.

Emergency Exits

Exit routes engraved directly into floor concrete — visible, permanent, and readable even in low-light conditions. No more repainting every year.

Hazard & Walk Zones

Designate restricted areas, forklift zones, pedestrian walkways, and hazard boundaries — permanently marked and always visible on the floor.

Safety Assembly Areas

Muster points, first aid locations, and safety stations marked in concrete. Permanent identification that holds up in any weather or condition.

The Process

With or Without Epoxy

After engraving, you have two options for the finish:

  • —

    Epoxy Fill

    We pack the engraved lines with colored epoxy, then sand flush for a smooth, vibrant design. Great for logos, text, and anything you want to really stand out.

  • —

    Natural Engraved Finish

    Clean, sharp lines with no fill. A more subtle look that works well for large patterns, borders, and textured designs.
